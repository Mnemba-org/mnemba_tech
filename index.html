Social Media Automation

Your website can create and publish your social content automatically.

Connect your website to an intelligent content system that continuously finds updates, creates social media content and publishes it according to your schedule.

📱

Automated Social Media Content

I build intelligent systems that connect to your website, automatically pull your business information, turn it into engaging social media content, and publish it for you.

🌐 Pull Information — Collect information directly from your website.
🔎 Detect Updates — Identify new products, services, articles, offers, or other updates.
🤖 Generate Content — Use AI to create captions, posts, images, and videos.
📅 Choose Schedule — Daily, every 2 days, weekly, or a custom schedule.
🚀 Auto Publish — Automatically publish the generated content to connected social media platforms.
🌐 Website → 🔎 Pull Data → 🤖 AI → 📝 Content → 📅 Schedule → 📱 Auto Post
Example: Imagine you own an online shop. You add a new product to your website. The system automatically detects the new product, collects its information, uses AI to create a social media post or video, and publishes it according to your chosen schedule.
